Zedu, three playful directions

The same Zedu homepage in three styles borrowed from well-loved sites. Open each on your phone and pick a favourite.

🦉

Duolingo-style

Zee, a friendly chat-bubble mascot, waves and reacts when tapped. Chunky buttons and a "set up your cohort in 4 steps" progress bar.

fun, energetic
🌙

Headspace-style

Soft pastel colours and round characters that slowly breathe. A one-tap breathing guide: "Fewer tools. Calmer cohorts."

calm, gentle
✈️

Paper Planes-style

A dreamy sky. Stamp your city and throw a paper-plane hello. Your cohort "catches" it as a message.

dreamy, interactive
