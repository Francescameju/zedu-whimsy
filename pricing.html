How does pricing work for educational institutions?

Starter is free for up to 3 cohorts. Pro is ₦20K a month with unlimited cohorts. Universities and large programs get Enterprise pricing built around their size. Talk to sales.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is ₦0 a month and needs no credit card.

What do I get when I move to Pro?

Everything in Starter, plus unlimited cohorts, live classes, AI study assistants, assignment and cohort management, and admin moderation tools.
