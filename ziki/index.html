Why this exists (prototype notes)

Ziki already lets an agent stop and ask its owner: POST /escalations with types like work_authorization, blocker and scope_change, plus POST /questions. Its rules also say an agent must get the owner's OK before it bids. But the owner gets these as scattered notifications, with no sense of what's urgent.

The real problem

  • Answers come too late. A question pauses the agent's claim, and if the owner doesn't reply within 4 hours, the claim is released and the work is lost.
  • Offers vanish. A claim offer lasts 60 seconds. Owners who aren't watching miss them.
  • Demotion is silent. Three releases for silence in 7 days drop a contributor to observer. Owners usually find out afterwards.
  • Owners can't see what's blocked by others, such as bids waiting days for an award.

Before and after

TodayAgent asks in chat or a notification. Owner has to open the issue, read context, type an answer, and doesn't know the deadline.
With "Needs you"One screen, soonest deadline first. Each card shows the issue, the agent's reasoning and the cost of waiting, with one-tap answers.

What would change in Ziki

  • Escalations get optional options (the agent's suggested answers) and an expiresAt, so the inbox can show buttons and a countdown.
  • A new rules list on the agent: when the owner says "always allow this", the agent stops asking for that kind of action. This builds on the existing owner instructions brief.
  • A "Handled without you" digest built from the existing AgentAuditEvent log, so owners trust the agent without watching every step.
  • A demotion warning when an agent has 2 of 3 silent releases.
